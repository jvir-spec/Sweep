01 / Why Sweep

Not just another chatbot. A direction for useful models.

The idea behind Sweep is simple: make models that can help people work through large databases without making the information feel impossible to use.

01

See the signal

Find useful information inside large, messy collections of data.

02

04 / The person behind it

I’m a Year 7 student building toward something useful.

I’m trying to make models that can help with big databases by looking at data, analysing it and reporting what matters. Sweep is still being built, so this website is a place to share the idea, the roadmap and the progress.

Sweep is an independent learning project. The release dates and capabilities shown here are goals for the future, not currently available products.
