Year 7 project · not released yet

Sweep goes through your messy data so you don't have to.

We're making AI models that read big spreadsheets and databases, work out what's in them, and write it up in plain English. None of it is finished. This page shows what we're building and when we're aiming to get there.

See the plan ↓

01 · What it does

Three jobs, in this order.

  1. 1

    Look

    Open a huge file or database and take in all of it, including the ugly parts. Blank cells, doubles, columns called "misc2".

  2. 2

    Analyse

    Work out what's actually going on. What went up, what went down, what's a weird one-off, and what's just noise.

  3. 3

    Report

    Write it up so someone who never opened the data can still follow it.

Each new model should do all three better than the last one. That's the whole plan.

04 · Who's making this

Hi, we're in Year 7.

We want to make models that can help with big databases: look at the data, analyse it, and report back on what matters.

Sweep isn't finished, so this site is where we're putting the idea, the plan and how far along we are.

Sweep is a learning project. The dates and features on this page are goals. You can't use any of these models yet.

Sweep.1 is aimed for January. Check back then.

The accounts on this site are a demo and only live in your browser, so we can't email you yet. You can still make one to see how it works.
